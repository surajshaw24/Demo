Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pre-wedding photography services

How far in advance should we book our pre-wedding shoot? +
We recommend booking 3-6 months in advance, especially for popular destinations like Udaipur, Goa, or during peak wedding season (November-February). This ensures we can secure your preferred dates and locations.
What should we wear for the pre-wedding shoot? +
We recommend 2-3 outfit changes. Bring both traditional (saree/lehenga, sherwani) and contemporary outfits. Avoid loud patterns and logos. We provide a detailed style guide and consultation to help you choose perfect outfits.
How long does it take to get our photos? +
You'll receive a cinematic teaser within 48 hours. The complete edited gallery is delivered in 4-6 weeks. For rush deliveries (2 weeks), we offer expedited services at an additional charge.
Do you provide hair and makeup services? +
Yes! We have a network of professional hair and makeup artists across India. These services can be added to any package. Trial sessions are also available before the shoot day.
Can we include our pets in the shoot? +
Absolutely! We love including pets in pre-wedding shoots. Just let us know in advance so we can plan locations and poses that work well with your furry family members.
What if it rains on our shoot day? +
We always have a backup plan! We can reschedule, use indoor locations, or even embrace the rain for dramatic, romantic shots. Our packages include one complimentary reschedule for weather.
