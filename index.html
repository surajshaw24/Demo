How to Play

1. Set your bet amount using the controls

2. Choose a color to bet on

3. Wait for the result

4. If your color matches the result, you win!

Multipliers: Red/Green (2x), Violet (3x), Blue (4x), Yellow (5x)

Game Rules

- This is a demo version with no real money involved

- Results are randomly generated

- You can change settings like color mode and sound effects

- The game tracks your statistics automatically

About This Demo
