Our Story

Born in the heart of Dhanbad, Jharkhand, OMGKart began as a local dream with a national vision. Founded by Suraj Shaw, we have grown from a hometown favorite to a brand ready to deliver the best of fashion to every corner of India.

We believe that great style should be accessible to everyone. OMGKart is built on the idea that you shouldn't have to choose between looking good and feeling comfortable—or between the latest trends and your budget.
