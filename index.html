About Our Company

We are a leading logistics service provider with a strong presence in Jharkhand and Bihar, committed to delivering excellence.

Your Reliable Logistics Partner

We constantly strive to adopt new technologies and processes to make our operations more efficient and transparent. Our commitment to customer satisfaction has made us the preferred logistics partner for businesses across the region.

Our Modern Fleet

We maintain a diverse fleet of vehicles to handle all types of cargo with utmost care and efficiency.

Advanced Fleet Management

Our fleet of over 150 company-owned and attached vehicles includes open-body trucks, multi-axle container trucks, and specialized vehicles for different cargo types. We continuously upgrade our fleet to ensure reliability and safety.

All our vehicles are equipped with state-of-the-art GPS tracking systems for real-time monitoring of shipments. We've implemented a strict maintenance schedule to keep our fleet in optimal condition.

To enhance security, we're transitioning from open trucks to closed-body containers that are weather-proof, tamper-proof, and theft-proof. Our drivers undergo rigorous training to handle all types of cargo with care.
