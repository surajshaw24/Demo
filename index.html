GPS Tracking Features

Comprehensive features designed to give you complete control and visibility over your fleet operations

Live Vehicle Tracking

Track the exact real-time location of any vehicle on the map, including speed, route, and live movement status.

Route History Playback

View up to 90 days of route history, stops, movements, and timings. Know where the vehicle traveled and for how long.

Geo-Fence Alerts

Create a virtual boundary for any location. Get instant alerts when the vehicle enters or exits that area.

Over-Speed Alerts

Get notified instantly when the driver crosses the speed limit. Promotes safe and controlled driving.

Engine On/Off Status

Monitor engine ignition status in real time. Helps prevent unauthorized vehicle use.

Fuel Monitoring System

Track fuel level, fuel usage, mileage, and detect fuel theft or sudden drops.

Our GPS Services

Specialized tracking solutions for different vehicle types and business needs

Vehicle GPS Tracking Service

Our Vehicle GPS Tracking service allows you to monitor your vehicle 24/7. You can check live location, speed, route history, stoppage time, and complete travel details directly from your dashboard.

Fleet Management Service

A complete solution for transport and logistics companies. Includes multi-vehicle tracking, fuel data, driver behavior reports, geo-fencing, and custom alerts for full fleet control.

School Bus Tracking Service

A safe and reliable tracking system for schools and parents. View real-time bus location, arrival alerts, route playback, and overspeed warnings for student safety.

Fuel Monitoring Service

Advanced fuel-level sensors track fuel usage, detect theft, and monitor refilling. Daily mileage and consumption reports included.
