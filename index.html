Our Story

Premier Modeling & Entertainment Hub of Jharkhand

Founded and directed by Sonu Kumar Singh, The Iconic Eve is the creative force behind the state's most anticipated beauty pageant, 'Mr. & Miss Jharkhand's Rising Talent.' We are a full-service agency dedicated to crafting unforgettable moments, from the runway to your wedding.

Our journey began with a vision to create platforms that empower talent and build legacies. Today, we are proud to be Jharkhand's most trusted name in entertainment and modeling.

Our mission is not just to host events, but to create platforms that empower talent and build legacies. Every star that rises from our stage carries a piece of Jharkhand's spirit to the world.

- Sonu Kumar Singh, Founder & Director
