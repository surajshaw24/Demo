Our Story

From ₹3000 to ₹1 Crore: The Wow Idli Journey

Humne Dhanbad ke ek tier 3 city mein sirf 3,000 rupees ke investment se Wow Idli ki shuruaat ki. Ek simple bike, kuch basic ingredients, aur ek bada sapna - yahi tha humara shuruwat.

Har din hum bike par idli ka samaan lekar ghumte hain, fresh idlis banate hain, aur logon ko serve karte hain. Humne apni journey ko daily vlogs aur reels ke through document kiya, jisse local community mein humari pehchaan bani.

Ab humne ek challenge liya hai - 1 saal mein is chote se startup ko 1 crore ka business banana. Yeh sirf ek business goal nahi hai, balki ek proof hai ki passion aur dedication se kuch bhi possible hai.
